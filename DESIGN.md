# Design direction
Product register, restrained premium workspace. A business owner reviews payroll risk at a desk in daylight. Direction: 70% Ramp financial product structure, 20% Mercury typographic/surface restraint, 10% Linear interaction discipline. See docs/ui-reference-analysis.md for inspected evidence and limits.

Warm-grey navigation #f3f4f0; canvas #f7f8f6; white surfaces; charcoal #202825; muted #68736d; deep emerald #175d4b; border #e4e8e3; amber #9b641e; red #a13e3e. Use compact grouped navigation, a utility header and ordinary operational page titles. Four financial values form one metric band, with dividers rather than four decorative cards. Chart and obligations are the overview anchors; invoices and recent activity are real records beneath them.

Poppins 500/600 headings at 24/16px; Inter 13px body/tables and 12px supporting text; IBM Plex Mono identifiers. Tabular financial numerals. Controls radius6px, panels8px; no large promotional assistant panel, floating decoration or copied logos. Desktop sidebar collapses to icons; narrow layouts use an accessible navigation drawer. Cmd/Ctrl+K opens page search. Radix handles focus/escape for command and action dialogs. Transitions180ms, with reduced-motion support. Tables own their horizontal scroll; document body must not overflow.
