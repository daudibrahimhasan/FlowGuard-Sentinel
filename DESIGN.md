# Design direction
Product register, restrained premium workspace. A business owner reviews payroll risk at a desk in daylight; use a warm neutral canvas, white surfaces, deep green actions and amber cash warnings. Compact sidebar, generous working area, readable numeric hierarchy. Poppins headings, Inter body, IBM Plex Mono identifiers with system fallbacks. No glowing decoration. Financial chart is the main overview anchor; recovery uses an evidence panel beside structured proposals. Mobile collapses navigation into a horizontal strip. Focus rings and reduced motion required.
